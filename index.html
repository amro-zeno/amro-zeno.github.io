About me

Practical engineering for production and quality

I graduated in Industrial Engineering from Alfaisal University in 2026. My focus is making production processes measurable and consistent: defining the standard, recording what actually happens, and using that data to improve.

I work as a Production & Quality Engineer in plastics manufacturing. I run daily quality checks, track raw-material use and write product specifications. At university I studied real production systems, including a work-system study at a PepsiCo bottling plant in Riyadh.

I'm certified as an ISO 9001:2015 Internal Auditor and a Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt, and I'm a member of the Saudi Council of Engineers.
