I first walked into Al Takhassosy Plastic Factory in 2024 as a part-time trainee. I came back in 2026 for my Industrial Engineering internship from Alfaisal University, and I stayed on as an engineer.

My days are split between the production floor and the spreadsheet. I weigh trunking bundles to write spec sheets. I log the raw-material mix on every pipe product, and I trace machine rejects back to their root cause. Then I build the Excel tools that make the next person's job easier.

I'm certified as an ISO 9001:2015 Internal Auditor and a Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt, and I'm a member of the Saudi Council of Engineers. I'm looking for roles in production, quality and industrial engineering in Saudi Arabia.

PepsiCo Line Study

For IE 307 Work System Analysis, our team visited a PepsiCo bottling plant in Riyadh and charted its PET, NRB and can lines step by step. A full changeover stops a 120,000 units/hour line for about 4 hours. We proposed SMED, automated changeover and clean-in-place pre-flushing to bring it to 1.5 hours, raising line availability from 83% to 94%.
